V37.3 playback hotfix

- Keeps YouTube format 18 as the acquisition source.
- FFmpeg still transcodes progressively to 128 kbps MP3.
- MP3 output now starts directly with MPEG audio frames: ID3v2 and Xing headers are disabled for the live pipe.
- Resolver proves at least 16 KB of MP3 before opening HTTP 200.
- Live response explicitly advertises Accept-Ranges: none.
- Pair with the V37.3 Worker, which feeds the live MP3 to Chrome through MediaSource audio/mpeg instead of the native range-seeking loader.
